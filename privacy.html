Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

1. Complete On-Device Privacy

At Text to Handwriting, your personal privacy is guaranteed by design. The software is built entirely with client-side JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. All text conversion and image generation processes happen locally inside your browser memory.

2. Data Collection and Retention

  • User-Input Text: We do not log, inspect, transmit, or store the text you write in any language.
  • Generated Files: All exported PNG images and PDFs are created locally on your device.
  • No Account Required: You are never asked to register, sign in, or provide personal identifiable information.

3. Fonts & Third-Party Assets

Our multilingual fonts are loaded directly from the Google Fonts CDN to ensure fast loading speeds and reliable rendering across all operating systems.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because no personal data is collected or transferred to servers, Text to Handwriting fully complies with global privacy standards, including the GDPR and CCPA.
