Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. The Foundation of Our Privacy Architecture

Text to Handwriting Pro is designed around a simple principle: What you write in your browser stays in your browser.

Unlike typical web applications that upload input data to an external API or cloud database to process outputs, our software is written entirely in client-side HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. All font conversions, baseline jitter routines, and canvas image exports occur locally within your computer's or device's memory.

2. What Data We Collect

  • User-Entered Text: We never log, store, or transmit your typed or pasted text. It remains in client memory and is wiped the moment you refresh or close the tab.
  • Generated Images: Any rendered PNG or PDF is compiled directly on your device via the Canvas API. No copies are sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Local Storage: We do not track your user profile or sell browsing information to third-party ad networks.

3. Third-Party Web Assets

To provide high-quality handwriting fonts, our application requests open-source font glyphs via Google Fonts CDN. These fonts are loaded directly from Google's content delivery networks. For more information regarding their practices, consult Google's official privacy documentation.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not store, process, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), our software inherently complies with the core principles of both the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

5. Questions and Concerns

For questions or feedback regarding this policy or the software's architecture, contact us at privacy@yourdomain.com.
